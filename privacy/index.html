      Privacy Policy

      Last updated: 2026-11-14

      This policy explains, in plain language, what Toolora does and does not do with information. In short: we do not ask you to create an account, we do not upload your files, and we do not run analytics or advertising.

      Your files and text

      The tools on Toolora — including the Image Compressor, Image Resizer, JSON Formatter, Word Counter and Percentage Calculator — process what you give them inside your own web browser. Images, text, JSON and numbers you enter are not uploaded to our servers, are not logged and are not kept after you close or reset the page. Downloaded results are created on your device.

      Information we collect

      Toolora does not collect personal information through the site. We do not have user accounts, do not ask for your name or email address to use a tool, and do not set tracking cookies. We currently use no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts and load no third-party fonts or scripts.

      What is saved on your device

      To remember your preferences, Toolora uses your browser’s local storage. This is saved only on your device and is never sent to us. It contains:

      • your chosen theme (light or dark) and language;
      • the list of tools you marked as favourites;
      • a short list of tools you opened recently (you can clear it on the home page).

      You can delete this at any time by clearing your browser’s site data for this website. The site works without it.

      Hosting and server logs

      Toolora is delivered as static files by a hosting and content delivery provider (Cloudflare Pages). Like any website host, the provider may process technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested in order to deliver and secure the site. This is governed by the provider’s own privacy policy. We do not use this data to identify you.

      Feedback and contact

      The “Send feedback” form does not send anything by itself — it prepares an email in your own email app. If you choose to send it, we receive the message and the email address you sent it from, and we use them only to respond to you.

      Third parties

      We do not sell or share personal information, because we do not collect it. If we ever add analytics or advertising, we will update this policy before doing so and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent.

      Children

      Toolora does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The tools are general-purpose and do not require any personal details.

      Changes to this policy

      If we change how the site works in a way that affects privacy, we will update this page and the date below.

      Contact

      Questions about privacy? Email hello@toolora.example or visit the Contact page.
